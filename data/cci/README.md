# Ligand-receptor table

`human_lr_pair.csv` is the human ligand-receptor table (3,398 pairs) of **CellTalkDB**:

> Shao X, Liao J, Li C, Lu X, Cheng J, Fan X. CellTalkDB: a manually curated database of ligand-receptor interactions in
> humans and mice. *Brief Bioinform* 22, bbaa269 (2021). <https://github.com/ZJUFanLab/CellTalkDB>

It is read by **Cell-Cell Interaction Analysis** (QuPath preference *CCI dataset file*): the second and third columns
are the ligand and receptor gene symbols. Check the terms of use of CellTalkDB before redistributing it; a newer version
can be downloaded from the link above and used instead.
